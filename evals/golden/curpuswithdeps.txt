Refunds are issued within 14 days of purchase.
This applies only when the product is unused and sealed.
Opened items are eligible for store credit instead.
Store credit never expires and can be used online.
Warranty claims require the serial number printed on the box.
The claim must also include a photo of the damage.
Without both, the claim is rejected automatically.
Delivery to remote areas may take up to 21 business days.
Standard delivery to city addresses takes three days.
Express shipping is available for an extra fee.
Orders above 300 shekels ship free of charge.
This discount does not apply to express shipping.
Damaged parcels must be reported within 48 hours.
After that window, the carrier is no longer liable.
Refused deliveries are returned to the warehouse.
